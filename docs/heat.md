## Setup

- **Index:** apparent temperature (Steadman 1994, shade version used by the Australian Bureau of Meteorology): AT = T + 0.33 e - 0.70 ws - 4.00, from air temperature, vapour pressure and 10 m wind. The daily value is the highest hourly value in the IST day. Direct sun would add several degrees; the index does not include it.
- **ERA5:** hourly reanalysis at 0.25 degree, through Open-Meteo's historical weather API (no account needed). Over Mumbai the API serves the land cells at 19.00 N and 19.25 N, both at 73.00 E, slightly inland of the city. Each ward takes the nearer cell.
- **Forecast:** ECMWF, GFS and ICON hourly temperature, humidity and wind (Open-Meteo's archive of short-lead forecasts, so about a day ahead). The index is computed per model at each ward's point and quantile-mapped to ERA5.
- **Split by time:** correction fitted on 01 Feb 2024 to 31 Jul 2024 and the method chosen on 01 Aug 2024 to 31 Dec 2024; then the correction refitted on both and scored once on 01 Jan 2025 to 31 Dec 2025. The ECMWF archive starts in February 2024, so there is no earlier training year with all three models.
- **Probability** on the map is the share of the three corrected models at orange or above.
- **Scored per cell-day** (730 in 2025): the wards in one ERA5 cell share one truth, so their forecasts are averaged first.
- **Baselines:** persistence repeats yesterday's ERA5 value (better than anything available live, since ERA5 arrives about five days late); climatology is the 1991-2020 mean for that time of year.

## Limits

- The thresholds are statistical, not yet linked to hospital admissions or deaths; the Stage 13 alert rules should check them against a heat action plan.
- ERA5's nearest land cells sit east of the island city, where afternoons are hotter and less breezy than on the western seafront.
- Night-time heat (high minimum temperature) is not scored; it matters for health in humid heat.
- Forecasts are about a day ahead; longer leads will score lower.
- One year of archived forecasts with all three models is little to learn a correction from: the method chosen on August-December 2024 was not the best one in 2025 (see the two tables). The correction probably depends on the season.
