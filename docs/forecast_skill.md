# Forecast skill: daily rain against baselines

Generated by `python -m models.forecast --train 2024-06-01 2024-09-30 --test 2025-06-01 2025-09-30`. Do not edit by hand.

## What was tested

- **Forecasts:** daily rain from ECMWF, GFS and ICON (Open-Meteo), their plain average, and the average after quantile mapping learned on the training period.
- **Lead time:** past days come from Open-Meteo's archive of stitched short-lead forecasts, so these scores are for forecasts issued up to about a day ahead. Skill at two or more days ahead is not measured here and will be lower.
- **Truth:** IMD gridded daily rainfall (0.25 degree), the 24 hours ending 08:30 IST, interpolated to the 30 pilot grid cells. Neighbouring cells share the same IMD values, so the cells are far from independent.
- **Baseline:** persistence, which repeats the last observed day.
- **Split by time:** trained on 2024-06-01 to 2024-09-30 (122 of 122 days matched), tested on 2025-06-01 to 2025-09-30 (122 of 122 days matched).
- **Scores:** POD = share of observed events that were forecast. FAR = share of forecast events that did not happen. CSI = hits / (hits + misses + false alarms). Bias = forecast events / observed events. The CSI range is a 90% interval from resampling whole days.
- **Not done:** the pySTEPS nowcast baseline needs a sequence of recent rain maps (IMERG or GSMaP), which the project cannot download until the accounts exist. The ConvLSTM experiment is separate work on Kaggle.
