## Result

- **Season false alarms.** Days on which the model flags at least one ward, against the heavy-rain rule: 2024: model 81 days, rule 31 days (of 122), 2025: model 80 days, rule 24 days (of 122). Both flag every reported flood.
- **The model warns on 2.9 times as many days as the rule, so it is not yet better than the rule.** Use the rule if a warning is needed before the labels improve.
- **The final model uses one feature, `rain_overnight_mm`, cut at 8.9.** With only dry days as negatives, the labels teach nothing beyond "some rain", and tide and ward features add nothing.

## Read this first

- **The labels are few and unchecked.** 59 ward-days (37 flooded) on 13 dates. 0 label rows have been reviewed by a person. Every score below moves a lot if a handful of rows change.
- **The not-flooded ward-days are all dry days** (under 2.5 mm for three days). Any method that looks at rain gets them right, so the false-alarm scores in the first table say almost nothing. The season counts further down are the better guide to false alarms.
- **Rain is observed, not forecast.** Live use will feed the model forecast rain, which is less accurate (see `docs/forecast_skill.md`).
- **Decision support only.** Official warnings remain with IMD, NDMA and the state.

## Setup

- **Features:** IMD rain in the 24 h before 08:30 IST on the day and in the 24 h after it, IMD rain over the three days before that, the day's highest astronomical tide, and the Stage 3 ward features. IMD is a 0.25 degree grid, so neighbouring wards get nearly the same rain.
- **Tide:** harmonic fit to Open-Meteo sea level for 2023-01-01 to 2024-12-31; on 2025-01-01 to 2025-10-31, which it was not fitted on, the daily high tide is off by 0.10 m on average. Storm surge is not included.
- **Model:** XGBoost, 150 trees of depth 2, with rain, tide, low-lying share and elevation forced to push the flood chance in the physical direction only. A ward-day is flagged when the probability is 0.5 or more.
- **Baseline:** flag a ward-day when either 24 h IMD window has heavy rain (64.5 mm or more).
